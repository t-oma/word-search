### Local Storage Data

We store the following information locally on your device using browser localStorage:

- **Game Settings:** Grid size preferences, theme preferences, and other game configuration options

This data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

## 4. Your Rights

Under GDPR and similar privacy regulations, you have the following rights:

### Right to Access

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

### Right to Rectification

You can request correction of any inaccurate personal data.

### Right to Erasure

You can request deletion of your personal data.

### Right to Object

You can object to the processing of your personal data.

### Right to Data Portability

You can request a machine-readable format of your personal data.

### How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. For data stored locally on your device, you can:

- Clear your browser's local storage to remove all stored data
- Use the cookie management options in our application
